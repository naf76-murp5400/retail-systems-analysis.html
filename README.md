# retail-systems-analysis.html
## 1. Overview
Retail is financially fragile because its income is directly tied to volatile consumer spending and foot traffic.
The analysis shows that retail value is governed by threshold effects rather than gradual change: once sales or demand fall below key levels, residual land value turns negative quickly, while gains above those thresholds are uneven and difficult to sustain. 
Because these thresholds are high and easily disrupted, retail rarely supports land costs on its own. 
As a result, retail alone does not generate enough revenue on its own to be viable. It does however, become econommically feasilble when it accompanies other less volatile elements, such as housing or office real estate, as an ammenity.

## 5. Capital Allocation Reflection

| Topic | Insight |
|------|--------|
| Retail Role | Functions best as system-supporting infrastructure |
| When Public Support Makes Sense | Retail activates demand, enables housing feasibility, or delivers public goods |
| Risk of Misallocation | Subsidizing retail without system impact may divert limited public resources away from housing or meaningful urban development outcomes |
